Lyra is a standalone, general-purpose AI agent. Point it at your own models and it reads code, edits files and runs commands on your computer — and you can pick the conversation up on your phone.

## What it is

- **Its own agent.** The loop, tools, skills and MCP support are written from scratch. It is not a wrapper around Claude Code or Codex.
- **Your models.** Add as many providers as you like, each with as many models as you like.
- **Desktop and phone.** Desktop apps for macOS, Windows and Linux. The Android and iOS apps connect to Lyra on your computer, so both show the same sessions.
- **Open source.** MIT licensed, on [GitHub](https://github.com/kittors/Lyra).
