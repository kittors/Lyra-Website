Say "make a video of…" or "make this picture move" and the conversation model calls `video_generate`. It writes the brief from what the chat already holds, says which picture to start from, and hands it to your video model. A video is a task you wait for: half a minute for a short one, several minutes for a long one. When it's done, it plays in the conversation.

## Add a video model

<Steps>

1. Settings → Models → pick a provider → **Add a model**, and enter the model ID, such as `grok-imagine-video-1.5` or `doubao-seedance-2-5-260628`.
2. For names with `-video`, `veo`, `sora`, `kling`, `seedance`, `hailuo` or `vidu` in them, or spellings like `t2v` and `i2v`, the model editor sets **Used for** to **Video** by itself. For other names, pick it yourself. Video models don't show up among conversation models.
3. With **Used for** on **Video**, a **Video API** menu appears below it. It's **Automatic** unless you change it; see the next section.
4. Click the test button on its row. It really makes the shortest video the model offers, and that call is billed.

</Steps>

## Video API

Every provider shapes its video API differently. **Automatic** looks at the provider's address first, then at the model name:

| In the **Video API** menu | Picked automatically when |
| --- | --- |
| xAI (grok-imagine-video) | The address is on `x.ai`, or the model name starts with `grok-imagine-video` |
| Volcano Ark (Seedance) | The address is Volcano Ark's, or the model name has `seedance` in it |
| OpenAI video format (new-api and other relays) | A relay's `/v1/videos`. Kling, Wan, Vidu and the like usually come this way through a relay |
| Google Veo (Gemini API) | The address is the Gemini API's, or the model name starts with `veo` |
| Alibaba Model Studio (Wan) | The address is on Alibaba Cloud |

When neither tells, Lyra tries the OpenAI video format, then xAI, then Volcano Ark, and uses the first that has the endpoint. Pick one in the model editor and only that one is used; nothing else is tried.

<Aside type="note">
A video model with **Accepts images** turned off in the model editor can't start from a picture. To make a picture move, use a model that takes pictures, or turn that switch on.
</Aside>

## Which one is used

With one video model, that one. With several, pick the default under Settings → Subagents → Session → **Video model**, or choose **Make default video model** in a provider's model list; with none picked, the first one is used.

A conversation can use another one: pick it with the wand in the composer, or @-mention a video model in a message. It works the same way as [picking an image model](/en/docs/features/images/#which-one-is-used).

## While it's made

- The video's place is held at its shape by a field of drifting dots. Above it is the stage and how long it has been, such as "Making the video · 1m 12s so far"; the stages are **Submitting**, **Queued**, **Making the video** and **Fetching the video**.
- The percentage in the corner is the provider's own when it reports one. When it doesn't, it's an estimate from how long that kind of clip usually takes.
- A network hiccup between two checks doesn't fail the video: the task is still running at the provider, and already paid for. The card only reports a failure after half a minute of checks going unanswered, or once 20 minutes have passed without the video being done.
- Say "10 seconds", "vertical", "1080p" or "no sound" and the model passes it on. Each model has its own range of lengths, and the nearest one it offers is used.
- To start from a picture, paste it into the composer or point at one already in the chat (the newest is `#1`, the one before it `#2`). It becomes the first frame. Some models can also be given a last frame, or pictures to take characters and style from without fixing a frame.
- Up to four videos at a time.

## Watching

Videos play in Lyra's own player. While paused, there's a play mark in the middle, and a bar along the bottom with play, the time, a track to drag, sound, full window and **More**. While it plays and the pointer is still, the bar steps aside.

- **Full window** fills Lyra's window, and the video carries on rather than starting over. Taking over the whole screen is **Full screen**, under **More**.
- Click the picture and the keys work: Space or `K` plays and pauses, `←` and `→` jump five seconds, `M` mutes, `F` fills the window, `Esc` steps back out.
- Hovering shows **Remake with** and **Download** at the top right. **Remake with** is there with two or more video models, and keeps the prompt and the pictures.

Video files in the file pane play in the same player.

## When a video fails

The card says why:

| The card says | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| The task is gone | It expired, or the relay restarted. Make it again |
| Taking too long to finish | 20 minutes passed. The provider may still finish it; it was not cancelled |
| Stopped waiting; the provider may already have billed it | You stopped the turn. Lyra no longer waits, but a task already submitted may still be billed |
| Out of quota, a key that can't use the model, refused under the content policy | The same as for pictures; see [When a picture fails](/en/docs/features/images/#when-a-picture-fails) |

## Good to know

- Every video is a billed call to the video model's provider. Unless you ask for more, the model makes one; unless you ask, it doesn't make one again.
- Videos are stored with the pictures, in `~/.lyra/session-media/`, and are still there when you reopen the chat.
- Every video made in any conversation is kept in **Studio** on the left-hand rail; see [Studio](/en/docs/features/studio/).
