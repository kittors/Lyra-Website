The sidebar groups conversations by project, or lists every chat by date. `⌘B` shows or hides it.

## The window at a glance

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| Toolbar | Back, Forward and the sidebar toggle on the left. With one conversation on screen, its title and **Conversation actions** (⋯) in the middle, and at the right end buttons for the terminal, the browser and Git, plus the **Panels** menu (⋮) |
| Icon rail (far left) | Conversations, [Pull requests](/en/docs/features/pull-requests/), [Scheduled](/en/docs/features/scheduled/), [Plugins](/en/docs/features/plugins/), Studio and Vault; Settings at the bottom, with an update mark above it when a new version is out |
| Top of the sidebar | Search chats, Notifications (the bell), New chat |
| **Projects** tab | Conversations grouped by project, pinned projects first |
| **Chats** tab | Every conversation, grouped into Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days and so on |
| Bottom of the sidebar | Your motto, or the providers you've set up if you haven't written one (Settings → Personalization → Sidebar motto) |

Below 760 px the sidebar becomes a drawer, the rail goes away, and its places and Settings move into the drawer. Back and Forward also answer to `⌘[` and `⌘]` (`Alt+←` and `Alt+→` on Windows and Linux) and to the mouse's side buttons, except while you're typing. On macOS the sidebar lets the window's translucent material show through; turn that off under Settings → Appearance → Translucent sidebar.

## Projects

A project is a name and a set of source folders. The first folder is the **main folder**, and that's where its conversations run.

- **Create one** with the project button above the composer → **New project**. Name it and add folders. Once you have a project, there's also a `+` next to **Projects** in the sidebar.
- **Project instructions**: put a `LYRA.md` (or `AGENTS.md`, or `CLAUDE.md`) in the project and it's included in every conversation.
- **Right-click a project** to start a conversation in it, pin it, edit it, show it in your file manager, archive its conversations, or remove it.

Removing a project only takes it off the list. Its conversations are archived, and nothing on disk is touched.

## Conversations

- **Titles**: a short title comes from your first message; for longer messages the model writes a better one. Turn that off under Settings → General → Automatic chat titles if you'd rather not spend the tokens.
- **Hover** a row for the pin and archive buttons. Pause on it and a card shows the full title, folder and usage.
- **Right-click** to pin, rename, archive, move to another project, copy the working directory or a deep link, or open it in [split view or a new window](/en/docs/features/split-view/).
- **Delete** by archiving first, then deleting from the archive list or Settings → Archived chats. Deleting can't be undone; what the chat spent still counts under Settings → Usage.
- **Status marks** show when a chat is working, waiting on you, finished but unread, or failed but unread. The bell at the top of the sidebar lists those chats (except the one on screen) along with scheduled tasks that failed unseen; click a row to go there. If Lyra isn't in front when a task finishes, you get a system notification that opens the chat.

## Sorting

The sidebar's **List settings** offers Recently updated, Recently created and Manual order. Dragging a project or conversation switches to manual order; press `Esc` mid-drag to cancel.

<Aside type="tip">
Want a conversation to work in its own Git worktree, leaving your main checkout alone? A new conversation's composer has a **Worktree** toggle: press it before the first message and that conversation starts in a fresh worktree. To make it the default, turn on **Start new conversations in a worktree by default** under Settings → Worktrees. Worktrees go in `~/.lyra/worktrees`, not beside your project.
</Aside>
