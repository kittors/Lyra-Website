## Browsing and searching

- Pieces are grouped by day: Today, Yesterday, then dates. Each one keeps its own shape, so a 16:9 picture isn't cropped to a square.
- Videos have a play mark and their length in the corner. A picture that was made from another, or changed into another, shows how many steps its history has.
- Hover one to see what you said when it was made. The search box beside the heading finds pieces by what you asked, by prompt, or by conversation name.
- The conversation list folds away while you're in Studio and comes back when you return to a chat. To see it here, press the sidebar toggle; that holds for this visit only.

## Opening one

The piece is shown large on the left; click it for full size. On the right, from the top:

- **The conversation's name, the time and the project.** **Show in conversation** opens that chat, scrolls to the piece, and makes its frame flash. Download is beside it.
- **How it was made**, described in the next section.
- **Prompt**: what the image or video model was given, with a copy button. If the provider rewrote it, the rewritten version is underneath.
- **Details**: model, size, aspect ratio and how long it took; quality for a picture, length for a video.

`←` and `→` step to the previous and next piece, and `Esc` goes back to Studio. Videos play right there.

## How it was made

A picture is rarely made in one go: first a dog, then the dog in a hat, then a girl holding the dog. **How it was made** lists every step in one column, from the first picture on:

- Each step shows what you said at the time and what kind of step it was: **Drawn**, **Edited**, **Made into a video**, **Video made**. A picture you put in reads **A picture you attached**, or **Marked up the picture** when you annotated it.
- When one picture was changed in two directions, the other branch is listed after it, joined by a line on the left to the picture it branched from. The path to the piece on screen is drawn in the accent colour.
- Click any step to switch to it.
- A picture attached to another conversation and changed there, or changed in a forked conversation, stays on the same history.

<Aside type="note">
Pictures drawn before 0.9.27 didn't record which picture each change started from, so their history is worked out from the `#1` and `#2` used at the time. A step that can't be worked out says **original not found**.
</Aside>

## Change it again without leaving

Under the picture is a box. Write what should change and press Enter: the picture goes back to its conversation with your words, and Lyra switches to that chat to draw. Come back to Studio afterwards and it opens on the new picture.

- Press the pen at the left end of the box (**Annotate the picture**), circle what should change and press **Done**; the marked-up copy appears in the box. Then write and send, and the marking shows up as a step of its own. Send the marks without words, and the conversation opens with the marked-up copy in its composer, waiting for you to say what to change.
- If that conversation is busy, Enter still sends; the message queues behind the current turn.
- Videos don't have this box.
